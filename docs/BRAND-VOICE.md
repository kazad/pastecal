# pastecal brand voice

**pastecal is the calendar you can share in a minute. No login required, for anyone.**
Free for everyone who uses it. Pro for the person who runs it.

We sound calm, plain, helpful and quietly confident. Think Carrd, Plausible, Doodle: a tool that
respects your time and doesn't need to shout. 37signals' attitude, at a lower volume. We are not
writing a manifesto; we are helping someone get a schedule to their group.

Approved phrases: **"No login required."** **"Refreshingly simple."** **"Counts only. Never who."**

## Voice principles

| Principle | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| **Plain over clever.** Say the thing in the fewest ordinary words. | "Click any day to add an event." | "Start planning together in real time with seamless collaboration!" |
| **Calm, never loud.** No exclamation marks, no "Oops", no fake excitement. | "Calendar saved" | "Calendar created!" / "Oops! Something broke." |
| **Helpful when things go wrong.** Say what happened, then what to do. Don't blame, don't apologize at length. | "Couldn't save. Check your connection." | "Failed to load calendar. Please try again later." |
| **Quietly confident.** State what pastecal does. Don't compare loudly or oversell. | "With pastecal, the calendar is a link." | "The best calendar app ever made. No logins. Ever." |
| **Talk to one person.** "You" and "people you share with", never "users". Active voice. | "You can't add or change events here." | "Events cannot be added or edited by users." |

## Words

**Use, consistently:**

- **link**: what you send. Not "URL" in UI text (fine in Help's technical table).
- **calendar**: not "cal", "schedule page", "board".
- **edit link**: `pastecal.com/<name>`. Anyone with it can change the calendar. Not "read/write link", "editable link", "share link".
- **view-only link**: `pastecal.com/view/<id>`. Not "read-only link", "public link", "secret link", "View Only mode". (Code identifiers like `readOnly` stay as they are.)
- **editor link**: Pro only. The keyed link a Pro owner gives the people allowed to edit a locked calendar. Never use it for the free edit link.
- **calendar app**: Google, Apple, Outlook. Not "ICS", "iCal", "webcal" in UI text ("Calendar feed" and `.ics` are fine where the raw feed is shown).
- **Save & share**: the button that turns a draft into a calendar. Always this exact name.
- **Pro**: the paid plan. Not "Premium", "Plus", "upgrade to unlock".
- **No login required** / **Nobody you share with needs an account**: login is optional (Pro owners sign in). Never "No logins. Ever.", "No accounts for anyone", "No logins for anyone".

**Avoid:** "simply", "just" (as filler: "just click"), "seamless", "leverage", "utilize", "users",
"Oops", "Please" in errors, exclamation marks, "Failed to…", "Invalid…", ALL CAPS, emoji in UI.

## UI microcopy rules

- **Buttons are verbs.** "Save & share", "Copy", "Restore this version", "Get Pro". Not "OK", "Submit", "Yes".
- **Labels are nouns, not sentences.** "Default view", "Edit link", "Views, last 7 days".
- **No captions under self-explanatory controls.** If the label says it, don't repeat it underneath.
- **Errors:** what happened + what to do. "Your browser blocked copying. The link is selected: press ⌘C or Ctrl+C." Use "Couldn't…", not "Failed to…" or "Could not…".
- **Success:** say what happened, briefly, no exclamation. "Copied to clipboard", "Calendar saved", "Restored the version from Oct 21".
- **Tooltips** name the action ("Rename this calendar"), not the gesture ("Click to edit title").
- **Loading:** "Loading…" with a real ellipsis character.
- **American spelling**: color, behavior, canceled, organization, favorite.
- **Sentence case** everywhere: buttons, headings, menus, dialog titles. "Add event by typing", not "Add Event by Typing".
- **Numbers as digits**: "3 events", "7 days", "$24 a year". Time as "5pm" in examples, "5:00 PM" in settings.

## Privacy

Say it short and say it the same way every time: **"Counts only. Never who."** Expand only when asked:
"Stats count views, calendar-app followers and places. We never record who viewed your calendar."
Never imply more tracking than we do, and never use privacy as a scare tactic in a Pro pitch.
Edit links "are kept out of search engines"; calendars "aren't listed anywhere". Don't promise "private"
for a calendar anyone with the link can open: tell people to pick a hard-to-guess name.

## Money

- **Plain prices:** "$24 a year or $2.99 a month, per calendar." State the unit (per calendar).
- **No fake urgency:** no countdowns, "limited time", "only today", "prices going up".
- **No price-change promises** ("locked in forever", "early-bird pricing for life").
- **Free is real:** "Free, with no trial and no card." Never call the free plan a trial or "Lite".
- **Pro pitches are quiet and contextual:** one line where the need shows up ("Want only your team to edit? Lock this calendar →"), dismissible, not repeated.
- "Most people never need Pro, and that's fine with us." is the right attitude.
