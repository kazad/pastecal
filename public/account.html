New calendar

Upgraded and locked to you, at its own pastecal.com/@you/… link. As many as you like; they don't count toward your 5.

Short links

Your own pastecal.com/name for any of your calendars. It opens the view-only calendar; you and your editors keep the edit link. Change where one points any time.

      Delete account

      Erases your email, sign-in links and sessions. Your calendars and their events stay.

      You still have an active subscription. Cancel it in Manage billing first, then come back once it has ended.

      When your account is gone:

      • Paused calendars stay read-only for good. Nobody can reopen them, you included. To open one to everyone instead, open it and choose Open to everyone before you delete.
      • Open calendars stay editable by anyone with their edit link.
      • Your @links keep opening the view-only calendars for 90 days, then stop. Your @name is never given to anyone else.
      • Your short links say “This link has ended” for 90 days. Then anyone can take the names.
