About pastecal

Sharing a calendar should be easy.

No login required. Nothing to install. Pick a name, add events, and send the link. Everyone sees every change, in any browser or in their own calendar app.

Made for team schedules, clubs, families, shared rooms and trips. More than 3,000 calendars since 2021.

Free to use. Pro, for locking who can edit, pays for the service. Details are in the privacy policy.

Made by Kalid Azad (Better Explained, Instacalc). The code is on GitHub.

Feedback, ideas or something broken? Post in GitHub Discussions.
