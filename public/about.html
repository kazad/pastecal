About pastecal

Hi, I’m Kalid.

I made pastecal because I wanted something simple: a link to a calendar that’s easy to edit, without logging in or installing anything. Most calendar tools ask everyone to sign up first. I just wanted a link I could remember, send to a group, and be done.

It started as an answer to a ViaPrize challenge, and I kept going because I think it’s a good idea.

The goal is to be clear and simple, something anyone can use without being a technologist.

I like making things that hit a sweet spot between simple and powerful. BetterExplained is simple math explanations. Instacalc is a powerful, simple calculator. pastecal is an easy way to share a schedule.

It’s free to use. If you want more control over a calendar, Plus and Pro keep the lights on.

If something’s confusing or broken, tell me. I read everything. The code is on GitHub.

— Kalid
