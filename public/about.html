About pastecal

A shared calendar you can set up in a minute.

I made pastecal in 2021 because sharing a calendar with a group was weirdly hard. Everyone had to make an account, accept an invite, and figure out the sharing settings. Half the group never did.

So pastecal skips all that. You make a calendar, add some events, and send the link. That's it. People can look at it in their browser or add it to the calendar on their phone, and they see changes as they happen.

Since then, people have made more than 3,000 calendars with it: sports teams, families, clubs, shift schedules, and a few apartment buildings booking their rooftop.

How it pays for itself

Making and sharing calendars is free. If you run a calendar and want more control, like deciding who can edit, there's Pro. That's what keeps the lights on. No ads, and I don't sell your data.

Your calendars

Every calendar is backed up each day it changes, and you can undo recent edits. I count how many people visit a calendar, not who they are. The details are in the privacy policy.

Say hi

pastecal is a small, independent project, and the code is on GitHub. If you have an idea or something's broken, post it in Discussions. I read all of them.
