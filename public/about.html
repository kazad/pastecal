About pastecal

The shared calendar nobody has to sign up for.

Why it exists

Getting a group onto one calendar shouldn't start with everyone making an account. A team, a family, a club or a volunteer crew needs one place where the schedule is right, and a link to send.

So pastecal skips the sign-up. Pick a name, add events, paste the link into your group chat. Everyone sees every change, in any browser, and can add it to Google, Apple or Outlook Calendar.

Since 2021

More than 3,000 calendars have been made on pastecal, for sports teams, schools, care schedules, shared rooms, shifts and trips.

How it's paid for

Making and sharing calendars is free, with no trial and no card. Pro, for people who run a calendar and want to lock who can edit, pays for the service. There are no ads, and we never sell your data.

Your data

Calendars are backed up every day they change, and recent changes can be undone. Edit links are kept out of search engines. Visits are counted, never who visited. The details are in the privacy policy.

Independent

pastecal is independent and self-funded, so it answers to the people who use it. The code is public on GitHub.

Contact

Questions, problems or ideas go to a real person: support@pastecal.com.
