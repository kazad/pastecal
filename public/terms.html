Terms

Last updated October 23, 2026

pastecal is free to use, with an optional paid plan, Pro. By using it, you agree to these terms.

Your calendars

What you put in a calendar is yours. You're responsible for it, and for who you share its links with. Anyone with the read/write link can change the calendar, so share that link carefully.

Pro (paid plan)

Pro is a paid plan for one calendar. It locks the calendar so only you, the owner, and people with your private editor link can change it. Everyone else can still view it. A Pro calendar shows one small “Made with pastecal” line, with a link to report it. You sign in with a link we email you; only the owner has an account.

Pro costs $24 a year or $2.99 a month per calendar, paid up front. There is no free trial. Teams, a plan for up to 10 calendars with no pastecal branding, will cost $290 a year or $29 a month when it opens. It renews automatically at the end of each year or month until you cancel. Payments are processed by Stripe. Taxes may apply where you live.

You can cancel any time under Manage billing. Pro then lasts until the end of the period you paid for. After that, your calendar stays with all its events and goes back to the free plan, unlocked, so anyone with the edit link can edit it again.

If a payment fails, Stripe will retry it. Your calendar stays locked while the payment is past due. If the subscription ends unpaid, the calendar goes back to the free plan, unlocked.

We may change Pro's price. If we do, we'll email you before your next renewal, and the new price applies from that renewal on.

We may end Pro on a calendar that misuses it, for example by locking a calendar that isn't yours. If you think someone has locked your calendar, write to support@pastecal.com. We may move ownership to the right person.

Fair use

Don't use pastecal for anything illegal, for spam, or to harm other people or the service. We may remove calendars that do, and we limit how fast any one browser can save.

No guarantees

We work to keep pastecal running and your calendars backed up, but it's provided as is, without warranties. Keep your own copy of anything you can't afford to lose: every calendar has a calendar app link you can subscribe to.

Changes

We may change the service or these terms. If a change affects you in a big way, we'll say so on the site.
