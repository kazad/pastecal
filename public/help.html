Help

Quick answers. Anything else: support@pastecal.com.

Make a calendar

Click any day and type an event, or press ⌘ E and type something like "Practice Tuesday 5pm". When it looks right, press Save & share and pick a name. Until then the calendar is a draft, saved only in this browser.

Share it

Every calendar has two links. The edit link (pastecal.com/your-name) is for people who help run it: anyone with it can add and change events. The read-only link is for everyone else: they see every change but can't edit. Both are in Share, next to the calendar.

Keep it private

Calendars aren't listed anywhere, and edit links are kept out of search engines, so only people with a link can open one. For a private calendar, pick a hard-to-guess name. To stop anyone else from editing, Pro locks the calendar to you and the people you choose.

Undo a change

Press ⌘ Z (Ctrl Z on Windows), or open Activity next to the calendar to see recent changes and undo any of them. Calendars are also backed up every day they change.

On your phone

pastecal works in any phone browser, with nothing to install. To see events in your phone's own calendar, with its reminders, open Share and choose Add to a calendar app: Google, Apple or Outlook. Changes show up there on their own (Google checks every few hours).

Several calendars

Click the pastecal logo to jump between the calendars you've opened in this browser, or to start a new one.

Put it on a website

Use the read-only link in an iframe and visitors see the calendar as it changes: <iframe src="https://pastecal.com/view/your-view-id" width="100%" height="600" style="border:0"></iframe>

Keyboard shortcuts

⌘ E
Add an event by typing
⌘ Z
Undo your last change
Esc
Close a popup or panel
