Questions

Does everyone I share with need to pay?

No. Only the person who runs the calendar pays. Everyone else uses it free, with no account.

Which plan do I need?

Free if anyone with the link can edit. Pro One to lock a single calendar. Pro once you run two or more, or want your own name. Team for a group with several owners.

What happens if I cancel?

Your calendars stay up with everything in them. Locked ones unlock and go back to the free plan.

Can I upgrade from Pro One to Pro?

Anytime. What you've paid is credited toward Pro.

Do I need an account?

Only the person who pays, so we know who owns what. It's made from the email you give at checkout, and you sign in with an emailed link, no password.

Can I see who looked at my calendar?

No. Stats are counts and places, never names or who viewed what.

What happens to my existing links?

They keep working. Moving a calendar under your @name leaves the old link pointing to it.

Will the price change?

Not for you. Early subscribers keep their price for as long as they stay subscribed.
