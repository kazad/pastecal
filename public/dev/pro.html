Questions

Do people need an account to see the calendar?
No. Viewers never sign in — that is the whole point of pastecal. Only the person who manages the calendar has an account.
What happens if I stop paying?
Nothing is deleted or locked away. Your calendar stays exactly where it is and the link keeps working; the Pro features simply switch off. We are never holding your schedule hostage.
Why per year and not per month?
One payment a year, no card to re-enter, nothing to cancel by a deadline. It is also how we keep this cheap to run, which is how it stays cheap to buy.
Can I pay by invoice?
Yes. Leave your email and mention it — plenty of institutions cannot use a card, and that should not stop you.
