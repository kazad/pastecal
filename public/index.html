A calendar you can share in a minute.

No login required, nothing to install. Add events, press Save & share, and send the link.

For teams, clubs, families and trips. Anyone with the edit link can add and change events, and everyone sees each change right away.

How do I share a calendar?

Pick a name like pastecal.com/summer-trip when you save. Anyone with that edit link can change the calendar. Send the view-only link to people who only need to look.

Is this private?

Calendars aren't listed anywhere, so only people with a link can open one. For a private calendar, pick a hard-to-guess name. To share widely, use the view-only link.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes, in any phone browser. For reminders, open Share and add the calendar to your phone's calendar app.

How do I manage multiple calendars?

Click the pastecal logo to jump between your calendars or start a new one.

Ready? Press Save & share, top right, and pick a name.

Questions

Who can change my calendar?

Anyone with the edit link. Everyone else gets the view-only link.

Is it private?

Anyone with a link can open it, so pick a hard-to-guess name. Edit links are kept out of search engines.

Can I undo a change?

Yes. Click "Edited … ago" at the top to see recent changes and put any of them back.

Does it work on phones?

Yes, in any phone browser. There is nothing to install.

Is it free?

Yes. Free does a lot: unlimited calendars and people, no login required. Upgrade for control, like choosing who can edit, and full stats: see plans.

Can I put it on my website?

Yes. Put the view-only link in an iframe:

<iframe src="https://pastecal.com/view/ni191jkhnx" width="100%" height="600" style="border:0"></iframe>
