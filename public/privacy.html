Privacy

Last updated October 23, 2026

pastecal is a shared calendar you open with a link. You don't make an account, so we don't ask for your name or email to use it. This page says what we store, who can see it, and how to remove it.

What's in your calendar

We store what you put in a calendar: its title, events, notes and settings. Anyone with one of its links can see it. Anyone with the edit link can change it. So give a private calendar a hard-to-guess name, and send the view-only link to people who only need to look.

Edit links are kept out of search engines. View-only links can be listed if someone posts them publicly.

Where it's stored

Calendars are stored on Cloudflare, with a backup copy each day a calendar changes. While we finish moving from Google Firebase, a copy is also kept there.

What we measure

  • Google Analytics counts visits: pages viewed, the kind of device and browser, and an approximate location. It sets cookies to tell repeat visits apart.
  • pastecal also keeps its own counts, such as how many events were added. These never include names or what your events say.

What stays in your browser

Your draft calendar, your list of recent calendars and your display settings are saved in your own browser. They don't leave your device until you share a calendar.

The Pro waitlist

If you join the waitlist, we keep your email, the plan you picked, what you'd use it for and any ideas you send. We use them only to tell you about Pro and to decide what to build.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, and we don't show ads.

Removing a calendar

Anyone with the edit link can delete events. To have a whole calendar removed, or your waitlist email deleted, write to us through the feedback link below and tell us the calendar's link.
