Privacy

Last updated October 23, 2026

Your calendar

We store what you put in a calendar so it works for everyone you share it with. Anyone with its link can see it, so give a private calendar a hard-to-guess name.

No accounts

On the free plan you don't sign up, so we don't ask for your name or email. If you join the Pro waitlist, we use your email only to tell you about Pro.

Pro

If you buy Pro, you're the only one with an account. We store your email, to sign you in and for billing; your subscription status, which Stripe sends us; and your sign-in sessions, saved as hashes. Emails come from login@pastecal.com: the sign-in links you ask for, and one welcome email when you first lock a calendar, saying what Pro does and how to manage it. We don't send newsletters from it.

Stripe processes payments for us. Your card details go to Stripe, and we never see them. See Stripe's privacy policy. To delete your account, cancel Pro first under Manage billing so you aren't charged again, then write to support@pastecal.com. Deleting it removes your email, your sign-in links and your sessions. The record of what you paid stays with Stripe, without your account, for accounting.

Visits

We count visits to the site, using cookies, to see what's working. We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

Each calendar also counts its own visits, for its owner's Pro stats: views and visitors per day, how many people follow it in a calendar app, and which countries and cities visitors come from. We store only those daily counts. We never store IP addresses or browser details, and never who viewed what. To tell a returning visitor from a new one on the same day, we keep a short hash made with a random key that is thrown away each day, so one day's visitors can't be linked to the next. A city is shown only once at least 5 visitors came from it on one day, and a country once at least 3 did, so a place can't point to one person.

Removing something

To have a calendar or your email removed, write to support@pastecal.com with the calendar's name. Please email rather than post the link publicly: anyone with the edit link can change the calendar.
