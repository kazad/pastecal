Questions

Will the free plan change?

No. Making, editing and sharing calendars stays free and account-free.

Does everyone I share with need to pay?

No. You pay for the calendar, once a year. Everyone you share it with stays free.

Do I need an account for Pro?

Only you do, so we know who owns the calendar. Nobody you share with ever needs one.

What happens to backups on the free plan?

Free calendars keep 7 days of backups. Pro keeps every change forever and can restore any date.

Can I switch a Pro calendar later?

On Teams, yes, anytime. On Pro, email us and we'll move it.

Can I see who looked at my calendar?

Pro shows how many people viewed it, how many follow it in a calendar app, and which countries and cities they're in. It never shows who they are.

What if I stop paying?

Your calendar stays, with everything in it. It goes back to the free plan, unlocked.
