    Questions

    What's free?

    Making, editing and sharing calendars, with no account. Pro adds features for people who run a calendar.

    Which plan do I need?

    Free if anyone with the link can edit. Pro for one Pro calendar. Pro Plus if you run several. Teams, in development, for an organization.

    Why does Pro say Beta?

    Pro is in beta: features may change. Everything on the Pro card works today, except what's marked in development.

    Does everyone I share with need to pay?

    No. You pay for the calendar, yearly or monthly. Everyone you share it with stays free.

    Do I need an account for Pro?

    Only you do, so we know who owns the calendar, and it's made for you when you pay, from the email you give Stripe. Later you sign in with a link we email you, with no password. Nobody you share with ever needs an account.

    Is there a free trial?

    Yes: 30 days of Pro, free, with no card. While Pro is in beta, join the beta and we’ll email you when your trial is ready.Start it from any saved calendar’s Share panel. When it ends you choose: keep Pro ($24 a year), or go back to a free calendar.

    How do I cancel?

    Sign in and open Manage billing. You can cancel there any time.

    What happens if I cancel?

    Pro lasts until the end of the period you paid for. Then your calendar stays, with everything in it, and goes back to the free plan, unlocked.

    What if a payment fails?

    Stripe retries it, and your calendar stays locked meanwhile. If the subscription ends unpaid, the calendar goes back to the free plan, unlocked, with all its events.

    What happens to backups on the free plan?

    Every calendar is backed up daily, and you can see the last 7 days of changes. Pro keeps the full history and lets you restore any earlier version.

    What is Pro Plus?

    10 Pro calendars with short names like pastecal.com/soccer, plus as many as you want under your own name, like pastecal.com/@you/soccer. $60 a year or $6 a month. It's in beta: join the Pro beta and we'll email you when it's ready.

    What is Teams?

    Everything in Pro Plus, with up to 10 admins, no pastecal branding, a year of who changed what and invoices in your organization's name, for $290 a year or $29 a month. It's in development: join the waitlist and we'll email you once, when it opens.

    Can I move Pro to a different calendar?

    Email support@pastecal.com and we'll help.

    Can I see who looked at my calendar?

    No. Pro stats show how many people viewed it, how many follow it in a calendar app, and which countries and cities they're in. Counts only. Never who.
