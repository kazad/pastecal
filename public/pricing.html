    Questions

    What's free?

    Making, editing and sharing calendars, with no account. Plus and Pro add features for people who run a calendar.

    Which plan do I need?

    Free if anyone with the link can edit. Plus for one upgraded calendar. Pro if you run several. Teams, in development, for an organization.

    Why do Plus and Pro say Beta?

    Plus and Pro are in beta. Early members keep their price. Everything on their cards works today, except what's listed as coming.

    Does everyone I share with need to pay?

    No. You pay for the calendar, yearly or monthly. Everyone you share it with stays free.

    Do I need an account to upgrade?

    Only you do, so we know who owns the calendar, and it's made for you when you pay, from the email you give Stripe. Later you sign in with a link we email you, with no password. Nobody you share with ever needs an account.

    Is there a free trial?

    Yes: 30 days of Plus, free, with no card. While Plus is in beta, join the beta and we’ll email you when your trial is ready.Start it from any saved calendar’s Plus tab. When it ends, nothing is charged and it goes back to a free calendar anyone with the edit link can change, unless you keep Plus ($29 a year) or choose read-only in its Plus tab.

    How do I cancel?

    Sign in and open Manage billing. You can cancel there any time.

    What happens if I cancel?

    Your plan lasts until the end of the period you paid for. Then each calendar stays, with everything in it, and does what you chose under “If your plan ends” in its Plus tab: read-only (the default: everyone can still view it, nobody edits until you decide) or open to everyone, a free calendar anyone with the edit link can change.

    What if a payment fails?

    Stripe retries it, and your calendar stays upgraded meanwhile. If the subscription ends unpaid, the calendar keeps all its events and does what you chose under “If your plan ends” (read-only by default).

    What happens to backups on the free plan?

    Every calendar is backed up daily, and you can see the last 30 days of changes. An upgraded calendar keeps the full history and lets you restore any earlier version.

    What is Pro?

    5 upgraded calendars with short names like pastecal.com/soccer, plus as many as you want under your own name, like pastecal.com/@you/soccer. Short links and @links open the view-only calendar; you and your editors keep the edit link. $96 a year or $10 month to month: one subscription, and each calendar upgrades with one click from its Plus tab. Already on Plus? Your calendars move to Pro and Stripe credits the unused time.

    What is Teams?

    Everything in Pro, with 25 short names, up to 10 admins, your logo, no pastecal branding, a year of who changed what and invoices in your organization's name, for $490 a year or $49 a month. It's in development: join the waitlist and we'll email you once, when it opens.

    Can I move an upgrade to a different calendar?

    Email support@pastecal.com and we'll help.

    Can I see who looked at my calendar?

    No. You see how many people viewed it, follow it and where they are, but never who.
