Frequently Asked Questions

Do my viewers or members need a Pastecal account?
Never. That is the whole point of Pastecal. Your members simply open the link on their phone or laptop. They see your live schedule instantly without remembering a password or downloading an app.
Can multiple staff members or instructors edit?
Yes! You can share your private publisher link or admin PIN with any fellow coordinators or instructors. Everyone else who visits sees the clean, read-only version.
How does embedding on Squarespace or Wix work?
It’s as easy as copying one line of HTML code into your site's embed block. Pro embeds are 100% responsive, load in milliseconds, and adapt to light/dark themes.
What happens if I cancel or my subscription ends?
We will never delete your events or take down your calendar. If you ever cancel, your calendar simply reverts to an open collaborative calendar. You always own your schedule.
